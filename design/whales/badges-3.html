Non-color cues

  • NEW — bar starts at zero with a 3px navy leading cap overshooting the track, caption New, count reads — → 27.0M.
  • ADDED — a second solid segment extends past the retained block; caption Added and a + percentage.
  • TRIMMED — a 135° hatched, dashed-outline segment sits beyond the current end; caption Trimmed.
  • EXITED — the whole bar is hatched with a trailing ✕ end cap; caption Exited, count ends in 0.
  • HELD — a single unbroken solid block, no second segment; caption Held.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Draw the share count itself and let hatching stand for what was sold, so state and size are one mark. Trade-off: a single shared scale flattens small holdings — BRKR's 0.3M is literally below the drawable minimum.
