Non-color cues

  • NEW — leading fullwidth ＋, the word NEW in 700 uppercase.
  • ADDED — leading ↑, 700 weight, no minus sign on the figure.
  • TRIMMED — leading ↓, 600 weight, one step lighter than ADDED.
  • EXITED — leading ✕, 700 uppercase word, and the ticker itself struck through.
  • HELD — leading —, 400 weight, lowercase word — the only lowercase state.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Encode state in weight, case and a leading sign so nothing but type is needed and the table stays quiet. Trade-off: the quietest option is also the slowest to scan — nothing pops, so spotting one EXITED among forty rows takes a real read.
