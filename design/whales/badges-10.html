Non-color cues

  • NEW — solid filled circle in the gutter; word New under the value.
  • ADDED — up-pointing triangle; word Added plus a + figure.
  • TRIMMED — down-pointing triangle; word Trimmed plus a − figure.
  • EXITED — diagonal cross; word Exited; value reads an em dash and the sub-line reads 0 sh, was ….
  • HELD — hollow ring, the only unfilled marker; word Held plus 0.0%.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked on the sub-line.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Treat the filing as an index: a narrow marker gutter for state, dotted leaders to the value, everything else whispered. Trade-off: the quietest and densest option — at 10px the marker shapes are near the limit of legibility, and nothing here will ever catch the eye on its own.
