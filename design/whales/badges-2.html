Non-color cues

  • NEW — ✛ glyph and the word New in tracked caps beneath the ticker.
  • ADDED — ▲ glyph and the word Added; magnitude carries a + sign.
  • TRIMMED — ▼ glyph and the word Trimmed; magnitude carries a − sign.
  • EXITED — ✕ glyph and the word Exited; shares line reads 0 sh · was ….
  • HELD — — glyph and the word Held; magnitude reads 0.0%.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Move the signal out of the content area and into the row's left edge, so state is scannable as a vertical spine. Trade-off: a 3px rail is easy to miss on a small screen and offers no room for magnitude.
