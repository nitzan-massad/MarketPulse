Non-color cues

  • NEW — ribbon reads New; watermark ✛; Δ cell reads the word new.
  • ADDED — ribbon reads Added; watermark ▲; Δ carries a +.
  • TRIMMED — ribbon reads Trimmed; watermark ▼; Δ carries a −.
  • EXITED — ribbon reads Exited; watermark ✕; value reads an em dash and shares read 0 · was ….
  • HELD — ribbon reads Held; watermark —; Δ reads 0.0%.
  • Covered — ticker is a solid filled chip that is a link, with a › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is a dashed-outline chip in normal weight, followed by the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Promote each holding to a card so the state can own an entire corner and a full-bleed watermark. Trade-off: cards cost roughly four times the vertical space of a row, so a 41-position filing becomes a very long scroll.
