Non-color cues

  • NEW — outline tag reads New; first chip is a dashed em dash, so the sentence is — → 27.0M.
  • ADDED — outline tag reads Added; second chip is numerically larger; delta carries +.
  • TRIMMED — outline tag reads Trimmed; second chip is numerically smaller; delta carries −.
  • EXITED — outline tag reads Exited; second chip is the literal digit 0.
  • HELD — outline tag reads Held; both chips carry the identical figure.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept State the change in words and figures — old chip, arrow, new chip — so nothing has to be decoded. Trade-off: it is the widest treatment by far, and seven sentences stacked give the eye no shape to lock onto.
