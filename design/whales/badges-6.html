Non-color cues

  • NEW — eight-point cross in the tile, five outlined pips, word New.
  • ADDED — up chevron, 1–4 solid pips, word Added and a + figure.
  • TRIMMED — down chevron, 1–4 solid pips, word Trimmed and a − figure.
  • EXITED — saltire (diagonal ✕), five outlined pips, word Exited.
  • HELD — single horizontal bar, zero filled pips, word Held.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Split the job: an unmistakable shape says which state, a five-pip meter says how big. Trade-off: two marks per row is a lot of furniture, and bucketing throws away the difference between 16% and 49%.
