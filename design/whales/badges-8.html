Non-color cues

  • NEW — bar pinned to the full right of the axis with a ✛ end cap and the label NEW instead of a percentage.
  • ADDED — bar sits on the right of the zero hairline; label reads Added with a + figure.
  • TRIMMED — bar sits on the left of the zero hairline; label reads Trimmed with a − figure.
  • EXITED — bar pinned to the full left with a ✕ end cap; label reads Exited.
  • HELD — no bar at all, a single 3px tick straddling the axis; label reads Held.
  • Covered — ticker is a link with a solid underline and a trailing › chevron.
  • Not covered — ticker is plain text with a dotted underline and the words not tracked.

Source: SEC EDGAR Form 13F-HR. Positions are as of quarter end and lag up to 45 days. Long US equity positions only — shorts, bonds and non-US holdings are not disclosed.

Concept Give every row the same centre-zero ruler so direction is a side and size is a length, comparable down the whole column. Trade-off: it needs a wide dedicated column, and NEW / EXITED have to be faked as full-scale bars because they have no honest percentage.
